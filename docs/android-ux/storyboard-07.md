# Storyboard 07 — Delete / Trash Email

## Evidence reviewed

- `recordings/android/storyboard-7-delete-trash-email.png` (1080 × 2400) shows Trash with one conversation, a 30-day retention notice, **Empty Trash now**, and a bottom **Moved to Trash · Undo** toast clear of bottom navigation. This confirms the recovery affordance is visible in that frame; it does not establish permanent-delete confirmation, successful recovery, or gesture safety. The MP4 was not reviewed.
- `src/components/email/ActionBar.tsx` uses one trash icon for both operations: `trashThread` outside Trash and immediate `permanentDeleteThread` plus local deletion inside Trash. The normal accessible name is simply “Delete”; the mobile 48px target sits in a horizontally scrolling action row after reply controls.
- `src/components/email/ThreadCard.tsx` commits a left swipe past 75px directly to permanent deletion in Trash. It tracks horizontal displacement without vertical direction discrimination or touch-cancel handling. A diagonal scroll can therefore qualify as deletion.
- `src/components/ui/ActionFeedbackToast.tsx` positions recovery above bottom navigation, but Undo and dismiss use 44px minimum targets. `trashThread` in `src/services/emailActions.ts` restores to Inbox rather than preserving the prior folder, and both trash/delete success messages appear before action completion. `ConfirmDialog.tsx` uses 42px mobile buttons, initially focuses Confirm, and confirms on any bubbled Enter key.

## Three exact mobile-touch improvements

1. **Name each operation and gate irreversible deletion.** In `ActionBar.tsx`, use visible **Move to Trash** outside Trash and **Delete permanently** inside it; use those exact accessible names. Keep this action in the initial mobile viewport with a 48 × 48 CSS px minimum target, moving secondary tools into overflow if needed. Reversible move needs no confirmation. Permanent delete and Empty Trash must open a danger `ConfirmDialog` before any service/local mutation: title **Delete permanently?** (or **Empty Trash?**), identify subject or conversation count, explain **This cannot be undone**, and offer **Cancel** and **Delete permanently**. Make both targets at least 48px high with 8px separation; default focus to Cancel for danger dialogs and remove container-wide Enter confirmation. Prevent repeated submission while pending and show failure without falsely claiming success.
2. **Make recovery reachable and faithful.** Keep the toast above safe-area-adjusted bottom navigation, promote Undo and dismiss to minimum 48 × 48px targets, and allow the message to wrap at 320px. Show **Moved to Trash · Undo** for at least eight seconds after a successful or explicitly queued move; pause expiry during focus/pointer interaction. Capture the original labels before moving and restore that location on Undo, including archived mail, rather than always adding Inbox. On recovery failure retain a retryable action and announce the failure. Trash should also expose a visible **Restore** action so expiry does not end recovery; permanent deletion must never offer Undo. Report queued/pending states accurately and announce success only when justified by the action result.
3. **Make a swipe reveal an action, never permanently delete.** In `ThreadCard.tsx`, require horizontal intent (`abs(dx) > 12px` and `abs(dx) > 1.5 * abs(dy)`) before translating a card. Vertical intent keeps normal scrolling. In Trash, a qualifying left swipe reveals a 48px-high **Delete permanently** button; release only reveals it, and tapping it opens the same confirmation from improvement 1. Keep the row present until confirmation and operation success. Reset offset on touch cancellation, multi-touch, insufficient movement, or Cancel; suppress the synthetic row-open click after a recognized swipe. Preserve the existing ordinary-mail Archive action rather than silently changing its meaning to deletion.

## Acceptance criteria

- At 320, 360, and 412 CSS px, Move to Trash / Delete permanently is visibly labeled and reachable without horizontal toolbar scrolling; Undo, dismiss, Restore, and both confirmation buttons meet 48px touch sizing and remain clear of Android navigation/keyboard insets.
- Move from Inbox and archived mail, then Undo: each returns to its original labels/location exactly once. After toast expiry, Restore remains available in Trash. A failed move or failed Undo has readable feedback and no misleading success toast.
- Delete in Trash and Empty Trash perform zero mutation before confirmation. Cancel, Android Back, and dismiss leave all conversations intact. The dialog identifies the affected subject/count, communicates irreversibility, defaults to Cancel, and submits once. Permanent-delete success has no Undo; failure leaves a recoverable row and retry path.
- On a Trash row, test vertical and diagonal scrolling, a 74px left gesture, a qualifying horizontal swipe, touch cancellation, multi-touch, and a tap on star/pin. None permanently deletes or opens a thread accidentally. A qualifying swipe only reveals the named button; its subsequent tap still requires confirmation.
